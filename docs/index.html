RoboTALES

Learning reasoning-guided robot policies via task-aligned simulated futures.

Hanan Gani, Tejal Kulkarni, Madhoolika Chodavarapu, Nicklas Hansen, Manmohan Chandraker

Paper Code Checkpoints

Task-aligned imagination for robot control

From raw instruction to reasoned futures to actions.

Pretrained video generators are useful world-model backbones, but their imagined futures can drift away from the task and fail to remain action-relevant. RoboTALES closes that gap by decomposing instructions into subgoals, steering generated futures with a frozen VLM critic, and training the video generator jointly with an action diffusion policy.

Method

A closed loop between reasoning, prediction, and control.

01

Hierarchical LLM planner

A long-horizon language instruction is expanded into ordered subgoals, giving the video generator a milestone-level scaffold instead of a single compressed command.

02

Task-aligned video imagination

Stable Video Diffusion predicts future visual states conditioned on the current observation and the planner output, producing rollouts that follow task logic.

03

VLM critic steering

A frozen vision-language critic scores imagined futures against the goal and uses reward feedback to refine the video model's task-relevant representations.

04

Joint action learning

A 1D action diffusion UNet consumes decoder features from the video generator, while action-level gradients flow back into the world model in one training stage.
